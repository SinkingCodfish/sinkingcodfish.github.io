Puzzle Bobble — but make it roguelike.






CONCEPT


Wobbungoo is a 2D puzzle roguelike with strong inspirations from the classic Puzzle Bobble.


You control a character who shoots colored balls that explode when matched in groups of three or more—racking up your score with every combo.

As a roguelike, you’ll unlock power-ups throughout your run and progress from room to room by hitting the target score in each stage. Each run offers a fresh experience, with a variety of possible builds created by combining different ball types and powerful perks. Master your combos, build your strategy, and push your run as far as you can!

VISUALS

BUY, UNLOCK, COMBINE... REPEAT!



Buy new balls, upgrade them, combine them… experiment with different ball synergies to get as many pops as you can!

Open perk packs in the shop and wait to see if luck is on your side! You craft your own build!

Combos, evolutions, big numbers… pure dopamine!
