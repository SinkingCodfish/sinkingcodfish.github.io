Who are we?


We are Sinking Codfish Studios, a Portuguese game dev studio!


We are a team of six: four college friends, one of our professors and his brother. We've been working together for about two years and during that time, we discovered that working together in this field is a dream come true.

We share a life philosophy: + music + Wii console = the ideal work environment. Staying small but tightly-knit is the key to keep our productivity levels well above average.

FlatFat Fartfest



On November 19th, 2024, we launched our first project on Steam: FlatFatFartfest, a quirky, humorous adventure-platformer where you control Chuggy, a "little fat boy" desperately trying to climb a mountain of bizarre obstacles—while battling an urgent need for... release.

It’s essentially our take on the game “Only Up!”.

Whatta Frogs?


A social deduction game with frogs!


It’s our most recently announced game where players take on distinct roles and must complete the round’s objective in order to score points. Each player has a box, and inside it they will find apples, bombs, or... nothing at all!
